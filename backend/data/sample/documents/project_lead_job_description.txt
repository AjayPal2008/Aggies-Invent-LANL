Project Lead (Level 3) - Computer, Computational & Statistical Sciences (CCS)
SAMPLE JOB DESCRIPTION FOR THE PROTOTYPE

Summary
The Project Lead plans, delivers and reports on research and software projects of $1-10 million for national security sponsors. You lead a cross-functional team of 6-15 scientists and engineers, own scope, schedule, cost, risk and safety, and are the main point of contact for the sponsor.

Responsibilities
- Write and maintain the project charter, work breakdown structure and resource-loaded schedule
- Manage budget, contingency and monthly earned value reporting; explain variances over 10 percent
- Run the risk register and monthly risk reviews
- Integrate safety, conduct of operations and quality assurance into project plans
- Lead the team: set expectations, give feedback, resolve conflict, manage workload
- Communicate with sponsors and stakeholders, including delivering bad news early
- Manage procurements and subcontractors
- Make sure export control and information security requirements are met
- Run change control and closeout, and capture lessons learned

Required qualifications
- 5+ years of relevant experience, including at least 2 years leading projects or technical teams
- BS in a technical field; MS preferred
- Experience with project planning, risk management, budget management and stakeholder communication
- Ability to obtain and maintain a Q clearance

Preferred
- PMP or equivalent certification
- Experience with earned value management
- Familiarity with quality assurance and export control
